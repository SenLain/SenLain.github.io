For one of our school assignments, we had to record a podcast about an IT topic. I did this together with my friend Keith Clive Roose. We were joined by Francis Chlarie, the owner of SYBA where Keith did his internship. Having someone with real industry experience as our guest made the conversation much more valuable than if we had just talked between ourselves.

## The Topic

The focus of our podcast was the impact of artificial intelligence on employment within the IT sector. We discussed automation, the changing role of developers and IT professionals, and the new opportunities that AI creates. We also talked about the skills that will become increasingly important in the future, as well as the benefits, risks, and challenges that AI brings for both companies and workers.

Throughout the conversation, we touched on many different aspects of AI in the workplace. We talked about where AI is already having the biggest impact in IT today, and which tasks are already being automated within real companies. We also discussed agentic AI and how it might be used in fields like penetration testing. Another topic that came up was whether junior developers are being hit harder than senior developers, and which IT roles are likely to remain safe from automation.

We also explored the risks of companies becoming too dependent on AI, and whether European companies are moving faster or slower than the US when it comes to adopting AI tools. Finally, we had a fascinating discussion about how cybercriminals are already using AI today, from writing convincing phishing emails to automating attacks.

## What Stood Out

The conversation was eye-opening for me. I had never really thought about how AI affects junior and senior developers differently. Francis explained that junior developers might actually be harder hit because tasks that used to be learning opportunities, like writing basic code or debugging simple issues, can now be done by AI. Senior developers, on the other hand, focus more on architecture, problem solving, and understanding business needs, which AI cannot easily replace.

I was also surprised by how concrete the cybersecurity risks are. Francis gave real examples that made me realise AI is not just a helpful tool. It is also a weapon in the wrong hands.

## My Takeaway

Recording this podcast was a great experience. I learned a lot from listening to Francis talk about the real-world impact of AI on IT jobs. The conversation made me think more about my own future and which skills I should focus on developing. AI is not going away, so the best thing I can do is learn how to work with it, not against it.
