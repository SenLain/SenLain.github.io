This was my very first Tech&Meet. These events are organised by our school where industry professionals come to speak about specific technical topics. The session was called ".NET 10 Demystified" and the speaker was Kevin Derudder. I went together with a few of my friends from class.

## The Session

Kevin gave a fast-paced but clear overview of the new features in .NET 10. He described the .NET ecosystem as "one big family" where everything works well together. I thought that was a great way to put it, especially compared to other environments where things can feel more fragmented.

To keep the session interactive, he created a live demo using the Spotify API. He showed how quickly you can set up a real working application with .NET. It was actually quite fun to watch him code live, including the small mistakes and debugging moments along the way. It reminded me that even experienced developers have to troubleshoot just like the rest of us.

## What Stood Out

What I appreciated most was how Kevin balanced speed with clarity. Since the session had to fit into a shorter time slot, everything came at us quite quickly. But he never lost me. His explanations stayed clear and easy to follow throughout.

He also brought a lot of energy to the room. You could tell he genuinely cares about the topic and about helping people understand it. That made a big difference compared to more dry, technical presentations I have seen in the past.

## My Takeaway

I walked out of there with a much better sense of what .NET 10 can do and how it fits into modern software development.

For my first Tech&Meet, it was a solid experience. I am curious to see what the next events will bring.
