Not just knowing the material, but being able to share it in a way that makes others excited too.

## The Topic

This session was all about DeepSeek, a Chinese AI company that is only about two years old. Dimitri explained how DeepSeek is completely open source, which is a big difference compared to models like ChatGPT from OpenAI. He also showed us how DeepSeek manages to achieve very similar results and performance to ChatGPT while using significantly less computing power.

That last part really surprised me. I always assumed that bigger budgets and more powerful hardware were the only ways to compete in AI. But DeepSeek proves that is not true.

## Why This Matters

Dimitri explained that the US currently leads the AI world, driven by giants like OpenAI and Google and supported by superior chips. However, Chinese companies like DeepSeek are advancing rapidly despite having less computing power available to them. Necessity, as they say, is the mother of invention.

DeepSeek did not challenge the big players with huge budgets. Instead, they succeeded through smarter optimization, efficient architectures, and strategic engineering. This was honestly inspiring to hear. It shows that innovation is not just about who has the most GPUs. It is about using resources smartly and solving problems creatively.

## My Takeaway

This talk reminded me that the AI world is evolving incredibly fast. DeepSeek being open source has also permanently influenced how AI is developed and shared.

But more than that, I walked away once again impressed by Dimitri. Sessions like this make me grateful to have teachers who care so deeply about what they do.
