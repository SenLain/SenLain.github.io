# About

Hi, I’m a third-year Cybersecurity student at Howest with a strong interest in penetration testing and offensive security. I enjoy understanding how systems work, finding weaknesses in them, and learning how those weaknesses can be exploited and ultimately secured.

## My Interests

A large part of my learning takes place on Hack The Box, where I work through realistic machines, challenges, and security labs. I particularly enjoy penetration testing because it requires combining enumeration, problem-solving, and creativity to find a path forward.

I’m currently preparing for my first cybersecurity certification, the Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS) from Hack The Box. As part of my preparation, I’m working through the associated Academy modules and labs to strengthen my practical skills in penetration testing and expand my knowledge of areas such as reverse engineering. I also want to start exploring binary exploitation and develop a stronger understanding of low-level vulnerabilities.

My goal is to keep building practical skills beyond what I learn at university and continuously challenge myself with more difficult problems.

## This Blog

This blog started as part of my university project, but I intend to keep developing it beyond my studies. It serves as a place where I can document my cybersecurity journey, write about things I learn, and share projects, challenges, and technical discoveries.

I hope it can also be useful to others who are learning cybersecurity or are just starting their own journey.

Outside of cybersecurity, I enjoy playing piano in my free time.
